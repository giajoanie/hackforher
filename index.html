About Us

Hack For Her was created by three high school girls from Mountain House High School after organizing MountainHacks, the Tri-Valley’s largest hackathon with over 200 participants. While MountainHacks was a huge success, one issue stood out: there were very few girls participating.

This inspired the creation of Hack For Her — a girls-only, virtual hackathon taking place from February 9–15. Our mission is to remove barriers like intimidation, lack of access, and fear of judgment by creating a supportive environment where girls can learn, collaborate, and thrive in tech.
